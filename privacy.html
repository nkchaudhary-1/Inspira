Privacy

Inspira is a new tab page. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data.

Last updated 27 September 2026

What stays on your device

Your tasks, notes, projects and settings are saved in Chrome’s extension storage on this device. The focus timer, your weather location and the last forecast are stored the same way. Nothing leaves your device unless you use one of the features below.

Chrome sync

If Sync with Chrome is on (Settings → Account) and you’re signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome carries your open tasks, items from the last 60 days, projects and settings to your other computers through your Chrome profile. Inspira has no server; Google handles this like the rest of your Chrome sync data. Turn it off at any time and everything stays on this device.

Calendar link (optional)

If you paste a private iCal link from Google Calendar, Outlook or iCloud, Inspira reads your events from that link to show them next to your tasks. It’s read-only, the link is stored only on this device, and Chrome asks your permission for that one calendar service first. Removing the link removes the permission too. Anyone who has a secret iCal link can read that calendar, so keep it private.

Google account (optional)

If you choose Continue with Google, Inspira asks for:

  • Your name, email and profile picture, to show which account is signed in.
  • A private app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), to sync your tasks, notes, projects and settings between your Chrome browsers. Inspira can’t see any other files in your Drive, and the folder isn’t visible to other apps.
  • Read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly), only if you choose Connect Google Calendar, to show your events next to your tasks. Inspira never creates, edits or deletes events.

This data goes directly between your browser and Google. Inspira has no server of its own, so it never passes through anyone else. Signing out removes the account from Inspira and revokes its access.

Inspira’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Weather

To show the forecast, Inspira sends the city you search for, or your approximate location (rounded to about 100 m) if you choose Use my current location, to Open-Meteo. No account or identifier is sent with it. The place name for your current location comes from your device’s time zone, so no other service sees your location.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are created on your device for focus sessions and task reminders. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Your control

  • Export everything as a file, or import it again, from Settings → Account.
  • Turn off Chrome sync or remove the calendar link there too.
  • Sign out at any time; removing the extension deletes its local data.
  • You can also remove Inspira’s access in your Google Account’s security settings.
